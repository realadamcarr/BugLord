# BIOSCAN-5M commercial rights review

**Roadmap task:** `BL-AUTO-F5BA623B`
**Reviewed:** 2026-08-27
**Decision:** Approved for BugLord internal dataset acquisition and model training

## Scope and basis

The approval covers BIOSCAN-5M images and metadata under the publisher's [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). It does not transfer ownership of the source dataset to BugLord and must not be presented as endorsement by the source contributors or institutions.

The copyright holder must be recorded as **CBG Photography Group**. Attribution must name **CBG Photography Group, Centre for Biodiversity Genomics** and retain the CC BY 3.0 reference.

## Operational conditions

Acquisition and internal model training are authorized only when the dataset manifest preserves:

- the upstream dataset/package version and canonical source URL;
- each available source URL or stable source identifier;
- the copyright holder and required attribution;
- the licence name, version, and URL;
- modification records for preprocessing and derived assets; and
- retrieval details and checksums required by the existing manifest process.

No approval is given to BIOSCAN-provided pretrained checkpoints or models; each requires a separate rights review. Taxonomy approval and BugDex mapping are outside this review.

## Residual release item

The legal and governance treatment of weights trained by BugLord on the approved source data must be revisited before any public commercial release. That residual item does not block internal model training. The machine-readable candidate therefore authorizes acquisition and training while explicitly withholding public commercial release authorization.
